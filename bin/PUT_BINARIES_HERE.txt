Place these two files in this folder:

1. yt-dlp.exe   — https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/latest
                  (download "yt-dlp.exe" from the Assets list)

2. ffmpeg.exe   — https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
                  (download "ffmpeg-release-essentials.zip", extract,
                   copy bin\ffmpeg.exe here)

ffmpeg is required for:
  - 1080p / 1440p / 4K downloads (merging video + audio streams)
  - MP3 audio extraction

If you skip ffmpeg, only single-stream formats up to ~720p will work.
