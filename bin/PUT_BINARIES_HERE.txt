Place these files in this folder:

1. yt-dlp        — https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/latest
                  Download "yt-dlp_win.zip" and unzip it here, so this folder
                  ends up with BOTH yt-dlp.exe and the _internal folder.

                  Use the zip, not the standalone yt-dlp.exe. The standalone
                  one unpacks itself into TEMP on every single run: measured
                  2.1-2.6 seconds of start-up per call against 0.9-1.0 for the
                  zip, and it leaves the unpacked copy behind whenever the
                  process is killed (which pausing a download does). 247 of
                  those leftovers, 1.03 GB, once filled a disk here and every
                  download then failed with a decompression error.

                  _internal must sit next to yt-dlp.exe or the exe will not
                  start at all.

2. ffmpeg.exe   — https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
                  (download "ffmpeg-release-essentials.zip", extract,
                   copy bin\ffmpeg.exe here)

3. ffprobe.exe  — copy bin\ffprobe.exe from the same FFmpeg zip

ffmpeg and ffprobe are required for:
  - 1080p / 1440p / 4K downloads (merging video + audio streams)
  - MP3 audio extraction
  - MP4 remux/recode compatibility conversion

If you skip ffmpeg or ffprobe, only simpler single-stream downloads may work.
